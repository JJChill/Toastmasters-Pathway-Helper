Pathways: Where Am I, and What Do I Do Next?

A one-sheet map of Base Camp (this page) and what to do at each point (page 2). The step-by-step guide with screenshots has the detail. Colours match the free “Pathways Highlighter” browser extension.The colours are added here to show the different kinds of item.

1 · The words (biggest → smallest)

PATH e.g. Presentation Mastery — your whole program
LEVEL Level 1 … Level 5 — a stage; do them in order
PROJECT e.g. Ice Breaker — a speech or assignment you give in your club
STEP Introduction and Assignment, Complete Your Project … — pages inside a project
REQUIRED “Level N: …” (Requirements and Checklist and Completion) and “Level N Completion” — they sit next to the projects, are easy to miss, and you need them to finish the level.

2 · What you'll see on screen

Left sidebar (inside a Path)
LEVEL Level 1
★ NEXT Level 1: Mastering Fundamentals ○
PROJECT Ice Breaker ✔
PROJECT Evaluation and Feedback ○
REQUIRED Level 1 Completion ○

Illustration: Base Camp doesn't label items this way. The coloured labels and ★ NEXT are added here to make the kinds of item easy to tell apart. Example only. The top of the page also shows a “breadcrumb”: Home / Path / Level / Project. It always tells you where you are.

Heads-up: a level can show a green ✔ while its REQUIRED checklist is still ○. Base Camp ticks “Level N Requirements” just for opening it. Always open the level and look.

3 · Find your next project in 3 steps

  1. Open your PATH from Paths & Learning and click Overview (top of the left sidebar), then Expand all.
  2. Find the first ○ that isn't done. That is your next item. (Resume also jumps to where you last were.)
  3. Before moving to the next level, check its REQUIRED items.

4 · “Am I done with this level?” checklist

  • Every PROJECT shows a green ✔ on the Overview. Each one ends with Complete My Project.
  • If the level has Electives, you've picked and finished as many as it asks for (Level 3: two).
  • If your level lists a Successful Club Series presentation, you've chosen one, given it and ticked it off.
  • Checklist and Completion: every item ticked, reflection questions answered, then Complete My Requirements pressed.
  • Level N Completion: choose your club and press Submit. It stays greyed out until every project is complete.
  • After the last level: finish Reflect on Your Path, then Path Completion and Submit.

Lost? Look at the breadcrumb at the top of the page or the extension's “You are here” box. Stuck? Ask your Vice President Education or a mentor before your next meeting.

What do I do when…?

Six situations you will meet, roughly in the order you meet them. “Guide” points to the numbered section of the step-by-step guide with screenshots.

1I'm new

  • Open your path, click Overview, then Resume or the first project in Level 1 (for example the Ice Breaker).
  • Read the project's steps in order from the sidebar.
  • Open Introduction and Assignment. Copy the Purpose onto your paper form and note the speech time. FOR YOUR PAPER FORM

Guide: section 1

2I'm giving a speech soon

  • On the project's first page, scroll to Resources.
  • Click Evaluation Resource and print it. 🖨 PRINT
  • Give it to your evaluator before you speak.
  • Practise to the speech time on your paper form.

Guide: section 2

3I've given my speech

  • Open Complete Your Project AFTER SPEECH once you've spoken and been evaluated.
  • Fill in the speech log, then press Save Log. (“Save & Email” also sends an email, so press it only when you're ready.)
  • Open Before You Move On and press Complete My Project.

Guide: section 3

4I've finished a level's projects

  • Pick and finish any Electives it needs. PICK ELECTIVES
  • Choose and give one Successful Club Series presentation if listed. REQUIRED
  • Checklist and Completion: tick everything, then Complete My Requirements.
  • Level N Completion: choose your club, press Submit.

Guide: section 4

5I've finished every level

  • Check each level's Level N Completion is done.
  • Finish the Reflect on Your Path project, ending with Complete My Project.
  • Open Path Completion, choose your club and press Submit.

Guide: section 5

6I'm lost

  • Open the Overview and click Expand all. The first ○ is your next item.
  • A grey outlined tick means not done. A filled green tick means done.
  • Ask your Vice President Education or a mentor before your next meeting.

Guide: section 6

Using the highlighter

  • The Pathways Highlighter box sits at the bottom right. Click its title bar (the arrow) to open or minimise it.
  • Press Alt+H to switch the colours on or off.
  • ★ NEXT comes from your last visit to the Overview. Open it again after you finish something.
  • It only reads and colours the page. It never changes your progress.
